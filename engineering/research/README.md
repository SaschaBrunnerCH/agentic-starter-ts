# Research records

The result of an `investigation` issue: a measurement, a comparison, an experiment. One file per investigation, named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`.

Each record states:

- the question and the issue it belongs to;
- the tested revision, and the environment where it matters;
- the method and the measurements, or where the retained artifacts are;
- the outcome, including hypotheses that turned out wrong;
- what would have to change for the question to be worth asking again.

A record is not rewritten when a later investigation disagrees; the later record names the earlier one. A decision that follows from a record becomes an ADR.
